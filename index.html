My experience at GitHub was nothing short of inspirational. Remote work was new for me, GitHub was new for me, and working with such a talented and established design team was new for me. Every dot on this page is a person from that chapter, distinct, colorful, and impossible to forget.

This art inspiration

Yayoi Kusama obsessed over dots her entire life. Dots on canvases, on pumpkins, on herself. For Kusama, dots weren't decorations, they were connection, repetition, infinity. She passed away at 97, and I found out on my birthday. That felt like something. Every person I met at GitHub is a dot in my own pattern. This page borrows her logic: the pattern only works because of all of them together.
